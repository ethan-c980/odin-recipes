Mac and Cheese Ingredients: - 1 pound elbow macaroni - 4 tablespoons unsalted butter - 1/4 cup all-purpose flour - 4 cups whole milk - 3 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese - 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese - 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese - 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard - 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder - 1/2 teaspoon salt - 1/4 teaspoon black pepper - 1/2 cup breadcrumbs

Instructions:

  1. preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Cook the elbow macaroni in a pot of boiling salted water until al dente, then drain and set aside.
  3. Melt the butter in a large saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and cook for 1 to 2 minutes to form a roux.
  4. Gradually whisk in the whole milk until smooth. Cook, stirring constantly, until the sauce thickens (about 5 minutes).
  5. Remove from heat and stir in the Dijon mustard, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper.
  6. Stir in the cheddar, mozzarella, and Parmesan cheese until melted and creamy.
  7. Mix the cooked pasta and cheese sauce together until well combined.
  8. Pour the mixture into a greased baking dish and top with breadcrumbs.
  9. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes until golden and bubbly. Let rest for 5 minutes before serving.
